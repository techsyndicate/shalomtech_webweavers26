WHAT IS A OWNERS CLUB?

We’ve been building quality bicycles since 1994, creating bikes that are made to perform, built to last, and designed to make every ride an unforgettable experience. For us, cycling has always been about more than just getting from one place to another. It’s about freedom, adventure, fitness, exploration, and the excitement of discovering something new every time you get on your bike. From your first ride around the neighbourhood to long weekend adventures, challenging mountain trails, and unforgettable journeys with friends, every bicycle becomes a part of your story. At Riders Avenue, we believe that owning a bicycle should be the beginning of an incredible journey, not just a one-time purchase. That’s why we created the Riders Avenue Owners Club — an exclusive community designed to make your ownership experience more rewarding, enjoyable, and valuable. The Riders Avenue Owners Club is our way of bringing cyclists closer to the brand they love while giving them more reasons to enjoy every kilometre. We want every member to feel appreciated, supported, and connected to a community that shares the same passion for cycling. Whether you are a beginner purchasing your very first bicycle, an experienced rider who loves exploring new routes, a mountain biking enthusiast who enjoys challenging terrain, or someone who simply loves cycling for fitness and fun, this club has something special for you. It is a place where your passion for cycling meets exclusive benefits, exciting opportunities, and rewards that make being a Riders Avenue owner even better. And the best part? Joining is completely FREE! You don’t need to pay a membership fee or make an additional purchase to become a member. All you need is approximately five minutes to complete your registration and unlock access to a world of membership benefits. We believe that being part of a cycling community should be simple, accessible, and rewarding for everyone. That is why we have designed the Owners Club to be easy to join and worthwhile to be part of.
